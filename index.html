ORIGO: Deep-Time Ancestral Atlas

In-browser bioinformatics platform and 4D geospatial atlas tracking 300,000 years of human evolutionary dispersal. Ingests raw consumer genomic variant arrays (23andMe, AncestryDNA, gVCF) directly in client memory to construct interactive phylogenetic trajectories grounded in peer-reviewed archaeogenetic literature.

  • Universal client-side genomic parser decompressing `.vcf.gz` using in-memory `pako`.
  • Phylogenetic resolver mapping paternal Y-DNA and maternal mtDNA branches to archaeological cultures.
  • Interactive 4D Three.js WebGL globe with dynamic temporal scrubbing from 300,000 BCE to 1900 CE.
  • Evidence Workbench correlating genetic claims with published DOI citations and radiocarbon-dated fossils.

Core Architectural Principles

Foundational engineering rules enforced across every web utility, Android bundle, and simulation engine.

01 / SOVEREIGNTY

100% Client-Side Data Sovereignty

Zero cloud telemetry. User bank statements, confidential legal contracts, tax returns, and raw genomic arrays never leave the client device. Computations execute locally inside browser sandboxes, Web Workers, or Android Scoped Storage.

02 / PRECISION

Mathematical & Scientific Precision

All financial calculators manage currency balances strictly as integer cents to eliminate floating-point drift. Genomic mappings resolve against exact GRCh38 coordinates and ISOGG phylogenetic trees. Simulation economies live in isolated, testable balance matrices.

03 / RESTRAINT

Tactile Restraint & Anti-AI Slop

No generic purple gradients, no floating clay balls, and no synthetic marketing platitudes. Interfaces prioritize WCAG AAA contrast (19:1 on headings), high data density, snappy 150ms state changes, and verified commercial vector exports without watermarks.
