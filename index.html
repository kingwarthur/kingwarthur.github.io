ORIGO: Deep-Time Ancestral Atlas

In-browser bioinformatics platform and 4D geospatial atlas tracking 300,000 years of human evolutionary dispersal. Ingests raw consumer genomic variant arrays (23andMe, AncestryDNA, gVCF) directly in client memory to construct interactive phylogenetic trajectories grounded in peer-reviewed archaeogenetic literature.

  • Universal client-side genomic parser decompressing .vcf.gz using in-memory pako.
  • Phylogenetic resolver mapping paternal Y-DNA and maternal mtDNA branches to archaeological cultures.
  • Interactive 4D Three.js WebGL globe with dynamic temporal scrubbing from 300,000 BCE to modern day.
  • Evidence Workbench correlating genetic claims with published DOI citations and radiocarbon-dated fossils.
