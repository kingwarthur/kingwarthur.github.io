## 🏛️ Architecture & Anti-AI Slop Design Invariants

- **WCAG AAA Contrast**: Headings at 19:1 contrast (`#0F172A` on `#FFFFFF` / `#F8FAFC`), body text at 9.7:1 (`#334155`).
- **Grounded Copywriting**: Concrete feature descriptions with zero synthetic buzzwords (*no delve, tapestry, seamless, elevate, revolutionize, empower*).
- **Surface Separation**: Crisp white cards with slate borders (`#E2E8F0` / `#CBD5E1`) and subtle contact shadows.
- **Data Sovereignty**: 100% client-side execution, zero cloud telemetry, and zero tracking cookies.
- **Real Screenshots**: 100% authentic captures from live sites, built Vite distributions, and Google Play store assets.
